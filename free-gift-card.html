3. Check eligibility

Why take a closer look?

Reward campaigns can be an easy way to discover promotions while spending only a few minutes reviewing the available options. The important part is understanding the conditions before starting. Never provide information that an offer does not reasonably require, and leave any page that asks for sensitive credentials or payment information unnecessarily.

Before you participate

  • Read the full terms shown by the offer provider.
  • Check whether the promotion is available in your country or region.
  • Understand any age, eligibility, or completion requirements.
  • Do not assume that a reward is guaranteed until the stated requirements are met.
